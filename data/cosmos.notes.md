# cosmos.json notes

Sources fetched: nomanssky.com/cosmos-update, PSU copy of the 7.00 patch notes, official 7.01-7.06 posts (all fetched), wiki pages Deep_Space_Outpost, Infested_Outpost, Asteroid_Belt, Ice_Field, Space_Hulk, Minor_Wreckage, Star_System_Map, Habitable_Asteroid, Deep-Space_Base_Computer, Space_Station, Gravitino_Coil, Dream_Aerial, Update_7.0x. Text is Spanish with English item names in parentheses where the official Spanish name could not be confirmed. Cosmos is free on all platforms including PS4/PS5/Xbox; console patches lag Steam (7.01-7.06 posts say "coming to other platforms"), so late fixes (7.04/7.05 limits) may not be on console yet.

## Corrections to existing objectives
- become_station_director: how -> Interact with the Station Core of a space station and choose "Claim Station"; the screen lists prerequisites. Wiki states 1 billion units are needed (community guides add a race reputation level and ~5 salvage contracts, unverified). Cannot claim Atlas Interface, Space Anomaly, Abandoned or Outlaw stations. Add requires "units>=1000000000" (https://nomanssky.fandom.com/wiki/Space_Station)
- complete_signals_tutorial_cosmos: how -> remove "(verificar disponibilidad en tu consola)": Cosmos is on PS4/PS5/Xbox; POIs show on the map once the save has visited the Space Anomaly (https://www.nomanssky.com/2026/09/cosmos-7-01/ and PSU 7.00 notes)
- equip_tractor_beam: how -> The Tractor Beam is a Corvette part bought at the Corvette Workshop in space stations alongside other corvette parts (PSU 7.00 notes)
- salvage_derelict_hulk: requires -> add "equip_tractor_beam"; how -> both a Gravitino Coil and a corvette with at least one Tractor Beam are required, not either/or (https://nomanssky.fandom.com/wiki/Space_Hulk)
- salvage_derelict_hulk and salvage_space_hulk overlap (same activity); consider merging.
- do_deep_space_outpost_contracts: how -> take contracts at the outpost "Salvage & Delivery Terminal" and deliver with the Gravitino Coil into the Salvage Depot (https://nomanssky.fandom.com/wiki/Deep_Space_Outpost)
- build_dream_aerial: repeatable -> true; how -> since Cosmos the Dream Aerial is a consumable that can be crafted repeatedly (3 Living Glass + 1 Solar Mirror), used with Relay Song in deep space; each system holds one living frigate (https://nomanssky.fandom.com/wiki/Dream_Aerial)
- recruit_organic_frigate: repeatable -> true (one per system) (same source)
- Starbirth chain (starbirth_* ids): Cosmos changed the Living Ship from the Starbirth chain to patrol around infested outposts and be actively sought (PSU 7.00 notes). Check the how texts of the Starbirth objectives that describe locating it.
